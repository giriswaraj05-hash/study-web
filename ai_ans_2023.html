✅ Questions & Answers
Q1
What is AI? Types of intelligent agents.
Answer
AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines. Types of agents: Simple Reflex (condition-action rules), Model-Based (internal world model), Goal-Based (acts to achieve goals), Utility-Based (maximizes performance measure), Learning Agent (improves over time).
Q2
BFS vs DFS comparison.
Answer
BFS: uses queue, explores level by level, complete and optimal, space O(b^d). DFS: uses stack, explores depth first, not always optimal, space O(b*m). BFS better for shallow solutions, DFS better for deep trees with memory constraints.
Q3
A* algorithm explanation.
Answer
A* uses f(n)=g(n)+h(n) where g(n)=cost from start, h(n)=heuristic estimate to goal. Admissible heuristic: never overestimates actual cost. A* is complete and optimal if h is admissible. Uses priority queue ordered by f(n). Combines advantages of Dijkstra and Greedy search.
