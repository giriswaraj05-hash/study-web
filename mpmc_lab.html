LABORATORY
Microprocessor & Microcontroller Lab

⚠️ Important Lab Rules

  • No entry without lab card/ID card
  • Fine will be imposed for damaging any equipment
  • Getting teacher's signature after completing program is mandatory

📚 Lab Theory - What You Will Learn

  • 8085 Microprocessor Architecture and Pin Diagram
  • Assembly Language Programming - Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication
  • LED and 7-Segment Interfacing using 8255 PPI
  • Working with 8253 Timer and 8259 Interrupt Controller
  • Stepper Motor and Traffic Light Controller Programming
  • Basic Programming of 8051 Microcontroller

📋 General Guidelines

  • Must be present 5 minutes before lab time
  • Wearing a lab coat is compulsory
  • Mobile phones must be kept in silent mode
  • Take teacher's permission before touching any kit
  • Write programs and make entry in the register
  • Arrange chairs and tables before leaving the lab

🔒 Safety Rules

  • Check all connections before turning Power Supply ON/OFF
  • Do not short VCC and GND - it will damage the kit
  • Inform teacher immediately if any IC gets overheated
  • Food and drinks are not allowed inside the lab

📊 Marks Distribution

  • Continuous Assessment: 25 Marks
  • Lab Record + Viva: 15 Marks
  • Final Lab Exam: 60 Marks
  • Total: 100 Marks
